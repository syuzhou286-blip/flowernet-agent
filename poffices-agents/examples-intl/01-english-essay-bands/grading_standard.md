# English Argumentative Essay · Grading Standard (Holistic Bands A–E)

This essay is graded **holistically**: read the whole piece and award ONE overall band — **A / B / C / D / E** — by matching it to the band descriptors below. Do not score sub-parts separately; judge the essay as a whole against each descriptor and pick the best fit.

Each band considers four things together: **Position/Thesis, Reasoning & Evidence, Counterargument, Structure & Language.**

---

## Band descriptors

### A (Excellent)
A clear, arguable thesis. Two or more reasons, each fully developed with convincing evidence or examples. A counterargument is addressed and effectively rebutted. Tightly organised (intro–body–conclusion), formal tone, varied sentences, virtually no grammar/spelling errors (0–2 minor).

### B (Good)
A clear thesis. At least two reasons, mostly developed, with some evidence. A counterargument is present but handled briefly. Well organised; formal tone; some sentence variety; a few errors (3–5) that do not impede meaning.

### C (Satisfactory / Pass)
A position is stated but the thesis may be simple or partly descriptive. Two reasons are given but under-developed or thin on evidence. Counterargument is missing or only hinted at. Basic structure present; tone mostly acceptable; several errors (6–8) that occasionally affect clarity.

### D (Weak)
Position unclear or only implied. One reason, or reasons that are merely listed without development; little or no evidence. No counterargument. Weak structure (e.g. one block of text); informal in places; frequent errors (9–12) that affect understanding.

### E (Very weak)
No clear position, or off-topic. No developed reasoning. No structure. Very frequent errors that make the essay hard to follow.

---

## Aggregation & rules
- **Overall grade** = the single band whose descriptor best fits the whole essay. When the essay sits between two bands, award the LOWER band unless the higher band's descriptor is fully met.
- **Scale (low → high):** E, D, C, B, A.
- **Pass threshold:** **C or above.**
- **Length rule:** target 250–300 words. If under 150 words, the essay cannot be developed enough to score above C.
- **Off-topic rule:** if the essay does not address *smartphone use during school hours*, it cannot pass (D or below).
- **Register rule:** persistent slang or text-speak caps the essay at C.
