# Student Submission · English Argumentative Essay

**Class: 9B　Name: Student A**

Phones are everywhere these days and lots of students bring them to school. In my opinion students should be allowed to use their smartphones in school because they are really useful.

First, phones are useful. You can search anything you want and find information fast, so you don't need to carry heavy books. Also there are many apps.

Second, phones are fun. At break time you can chill and play games and text your friends, and this makes school less boring and everyone is happy.

So basically phones are a good thing and school would be way better if we could use them whenever we want. Thats my opinion and I think teachers should let us use phones.
