# Mathematics (Grade 8) · Mid-Term Test — Questions & Full Solutions

**Total: 100 marks. Topics: integers, algebraic expressions, linear equations, word problems.**

---

## Section A — Multiple choice (4 marks each, 4 questions, 16 marks)
1. Simplify 3x + 2x.  A) 5x  B) 6x  C) 5x²  D) 6　**Answer: A (5x)**
2. What is the value of 2³?  A) 6  B) 8  C) 9  D) 5　**Answer: B (8)**
3. Solve x + 7 = 12.  A) 5  B) 19  C) −5  D) 7　**Answer: A (5)**
4. Which of these is a factor of 12?  A) 5  B) 7  C) 4  D) 8　**Answer: C (4)**

## Section B — Fill in the blank (4 marks each, 4 questions, 16 marks)
5. Evaluate −3 + 8 = ____　**Answer: 5**
6. Simplify 4a − a = ____　**Answer: 3a**
7. If 2x = 10, then x = ____　**Answer: 5**
8. The area of a 5 cm × 3 cm rectangle = ____ cm²　**Answer: 15**

## Section C — Calculation, show working (8 marks each, 3 questions, 24 marks)
9. Simplify 3(2x − 1) + 2(x + 4).
   **Solution:** = 6x − 3 + 2x + 8 = **8x + 5**
10. Evaluate (−2)³ + 4 × (−3) − (−5).
   **Solution:** = −8 + (−12) + 5 = **−15**
11. Evaluate (12 + 6 × 2) ÷ 6.
   **Solution:** = (12 + 12) ÷ 6 = 24 ÷ 6 = **4**

## Section D — Solve the equation (8 marks each, 2 questions, 16 marks)
12. Solve 3x − 5 = 16.
   **Solution:** 3x = 21 → **x = 7**
13. Solve 2(x + 3) = 14.
   **Solution:** 2x + 6 = 14 → 2x = 8 → **x = 4**

## Section E — Word problems (14 marks each, 2 questions, 28 marks)
14. A book costs $x. A pen costs $3 less than the book. Together they cost $21. Find the cost of the book.
   **Solution:** x + (x − 3) = 21 → 2x − 3 = 21 → 2x = 24 → x = 12.  **The book costs $12.**
15. A number is multiplied by 4, then 6 is added, giving 30. Find the number.
   **Solution:** 4n + 6 = 30 → 4n = 24 → n = 6.  **The number is 6.**

## Common errors (for grader reference)
- Q4: choosing a number that is not a factor of 12.
- Q10: sign error when subtracting a negative (−(−5) treated as −5).
- Q13: dividing 8 by 2 incorrectly.
- Word problems: writing only the final answer with no equation or working.
